--- title: "About" permalink: "/About/" layout: post categories: media --- I’m a molecular geneticist with an M.S. in Biological Sciences from the University of Illinois Chicago, where my research focused on the genetic basis of self-incompatibility in Cactaceae. My work combines laboratory genetics (DNA extraction, PCR, and microsatellite genotyping and allele scoring on an ABI 3730xl) with computational analysis in R, Python, and Linux, including RNA-seq, differential gene expression, phylogenetics, and statistical testing of genetic models. My path began with camping and canoeing trips around Wisconsin, which grew into a broad interest in ecology, especially plants. As an undergraduate studying freshwater sciences at UW-Milwaukee, I worked in an aquatic ecology lab studying invasive fish and molluscs as well as algal physiology. That work deepened my interest in photosynthetic organisms and introduced me to aquatic plants, and later to the diversity of plant mating systems. I’m especially interested in how mating systems vary across plants, environments, and pollinator communities, and how that variation informs the conservation of rare species and the management of invasives. I’m looking to apply my combined lab and computational background to genomics and data-driven research.

Outside of research, I play the guitar, bass, and saxophone. I love making music with friends, and we are always looking for new people to play with. I also enjoy gardening, cooking, going to the gym, fishing, and exploring!
